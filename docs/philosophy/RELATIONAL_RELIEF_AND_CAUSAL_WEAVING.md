## 1. Relational Relief Engine (`RelationalReliefEngine`)

### 1.1 Core Thesis: Relief Pattern vs Dual Entities
Classical intuition treats observed phenomena like particles and waves as two distinct physical entities. In ELYSIA, surface phenomena are mathematically modeled as **Relief Patterns (양각 패턴)**—projections arising from a underlying **Deep Tension Field** interacting with specific observation boundary conditions ($\Delta \in [0, 1]$).

- **Deep Tension Field ($\Theta$):** Continuous potential field defined by interaction potential $V$, connectivity conductance $C$, and phase momentum $\phi$.
- **Boundary Condition ($\Delta$):**
  - $\Delta \to 0$ (Wave Aspect): Preserves spatial phase interference, amplitude spread, and wave field continuity.
  - $\Delta \to 1$ (Particle Aspect): Localized detection events, peak impulse collapse, and energy gradient concentration.
- **Surface Relief ($S_{relief}$):**
  $$S_{relief} = (1 - \Delta) \cdot W_{aspect} + \Delta \cdot P_{aspect}$$
- **Inverse Parameter Extraction:** From the surface relief pattern, the engine extracts deep generating invariants ($\Theta$, topological entropy, MDL description length) rather than overfitting surface trajectories.

## 2. Epistemic Readiness Engine (`EpistemicReadinessEngine`)

### 2.1 Core Thesis: 'Ttae' (Season/Phase) and Receptive Capacity

- **Readiness Score ($R_{score}$):**
  $$R_{score} = \text{clip}(0.4 M_{soil} + 0.4 \cdot \frac{\cos(\Delta \phi) + 1}{2} - 0.2 B_{friction}, 0.0, 1.0)$$

  When facing an uncaptured causal residual, the system does not reject it as an exception or error. Instead, it perceives the boundary of its own frame ($B_{limit}$), transforming the gap into an open receptive space (**Yeobaek / 여백**).
- **Progressive Unfolding:**

### 3.1 Core Thesis: Multidimensional Intersection Matrix vs Linear Chains
Linear causal chains ($A \to B \to C$) flatten complex reality. ELYSIA weaves causal events across a 5D spectrum matrix:

1. **Frequency Axis ($f$):** Resonance and harmonic frequencies.
2. **Phase Axis ($\phi$):** Phase alignment and wave displacement.
3. **Spatiotemporal Axis ($x, t$):** Local atmospheric and spatial geometry.
4. **Cosmic / Scale Axis ($S_{solar}, S_{mag}$):** Planetary magnetosphere, solar wind, and heliosphere conditions.
5. **Relational Axis ($R$):** Network connectivity and global circuit coupling.

- **Local Micro Cause:** Cloud charge separation $\times$ atmospheric dielectric tension.
- **Macro Environmental Background:** Earth's magnetospheric flux $\times$ solar wind density.
- **Woven Discharge Potential:**
  $$P_{discharge} = (\text{Local Cause}) \cdot (1 + 0.15 \cdot \text{Cosmic Background})$$
